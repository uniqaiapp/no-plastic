coverage
  [ ] subject                  say who or what, with two or three physical facts (age range, hair, clothing, expression); 'a woman' gives the model nothing to hold on to
  [ ] composition              state the framing (head and shoulders, waist-up, full-body) and where the subject sits in the frame
  [ ] camera / lens            name the camera and lens so perspective and depth of field are decided: '50 mm at f/2', '85 mm at f/2.8', 'phone main camera, 24 mm equivalent'
  [ ] light                    describe the light as a photographer would: source, direction, quality ('large soft window light from camera left with a gentle fill')
  [ ] palette / grade          give the grade one clause: 'gentle filmic grade, creamy highlights, slightly lifted blacks' or 'true-to-life colour, no cast'
  [ ] texture                  ask for texture explicitly: 'natural skin texture with visible pores', 'fine film grain', 'fabric weave'; models smooth by default
  [ ] era / medium             anchor the medium and era: 'editorial lifestyle photograph', 'honest phone photo', '1974 Kodachrome slide'; without it the model picks 'render'
  [ ] anti-plastic negatives   close with what to avoid, phrased as a photographer would: 'natural skin texture, no retouched plastic look, not over-processed'
